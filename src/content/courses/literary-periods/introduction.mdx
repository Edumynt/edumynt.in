# Welcome to Literary Periods

## Course Overview

This course provides a comprehensive survey of the major periods in English literature, from the Anglo-Saxon era to the contemporary period. You'll explore how historical events, social changes, and cultural movements shaped literary expression across centuries.

## What You'll Learn

### 📅 **Historical Timeline**
- Medieval Literature (Anglo-Saxon to Middle English)
- Renaissance and Elizabethan Era
- Restoration and 18th Century
- Romantic and Victorian Periods
- Modern and Contemporary Literature

### 🎭 **Key Characteristics**
- Dominant themes and motifs of each period
- Literary forms and genres popular in different eras
- Language evolution and stylistic changes
- Social and political influences on literature

### 📚 **Major Works & Authors**
- Representative texts from each period
- Influential authors and their contributions
- Literary movements and schools of thought
- Cross-period influences and connections

## Course Structure

Each period is covered through:
- **Historical Context** - Political, social, and cultural background
- **Literary Features** - Dominant styles, themes, and forms
- **Major Authors** - Key figures and their works
- **Representative Texts** - Analysis of important works
- **Legacy** - Influence on later periods

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this course, you will:

1. **Identify** characteristics of major literary periods
2. **Analyze** how historical context shapes literature
3. **Compare** different periods and their unique features
4. **Evaluate** the evolution of literary forms and themes
5. **Apply** period knowledge to literary analysis
