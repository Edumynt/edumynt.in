# Literary Periods in English Literature

This course covers the major literary periods from the Renaissance to the Modern era, exploring the characteristics, key figures, and influential works of each period.

## What You'll Learn

- Historical context of each literary period
- Key characteristics and themes
- Major authors and their contributions
- Influential works and their impact
- Evolution of literary styles and forms

## Course Structure

This course contains all major literary periods in a single comprehensive chapter:

- The Renaissance Period (c. 1500-1660)
- The Jacobean Age (1603-1625)
- The Commonwealth Period/Interregnum (1649-1660)
- The Metaphysical Poets (c. 1600-1660)
- The Neo-Classical Period (1660-1798)
- The Romantic Period (c. 1798-1837)
- The Victorian Period (c. 1837-1901)
- The Modern Period (c. 1901-1945)
- The Post-Modern Period (c. 1945-present)
