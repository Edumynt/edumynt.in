# Literary Periods in English Literature

This course covers the major literary periods from the Renaissance to the Modern era, exploring the characteristics, key figures, and influential works of each period.

## What You'll Learn

- Historical context of each literary period
- Key characteristics and themes
- Major authors and their contributions
- Influential works and their impact
- Evolution of literary styles and forms
