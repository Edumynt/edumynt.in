# The Renaissance Period (1500-1660)

The Renaissance marked a rebirth of learning and artistic achievement in Europe, fundamentally transforming English literature.

## Key Characteristics

- Revival of classical learning
- Humanism and individualism
- Exploration of human nature
- Development of dramatic forms

## Major Figures

- **William Shakespeare** - Greatest playwright and poet
- **Christopher Marlowe** - Pioneer of blank verse drama
- **Edmund Spenser** - Epic poet of "The Faerie Queene"
