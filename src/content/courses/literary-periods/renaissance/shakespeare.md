# Shakespeare's Major Works

William Shakespeare (1564-1616) is widely regarded as the greatest writer in the English language.

## Major Tragedies

### Hamlet (1600-1601)
- Themes: Revenge, madness, mortality
- Famous soliloquy: "To be or not to be"

### Macbeth (1606)
- Themes: Ambition, guilt, fate
- Supernatural elements and psychological depth

### King Lear (1605-1606)
- Themes: Family, power, justice
- Double plot structure

## The Sonnets
- 154 sonnets published in 1609
- Themes of love, beauty, mortality, and time
