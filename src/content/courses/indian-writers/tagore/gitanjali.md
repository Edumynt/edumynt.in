# Gitanjali: Song Offerings

*Gitanjali* is Rabindranath Tagore's most famous work, originally written in Bengali and self-translated into English.

## Structure and Form
- Collection of 103 prose poems
- Originally 157 songs in Bengali
- English translation published in 1912

## Major Themes

### Spiritual Quest
- Search for the divine in everyday life
- Mystical union with the eternal

### Nature and Divinity
- God found in nature's beauty
- Pantheistic vision of creation

### Love and Devotion
- Divine love expressed through human emotions
- Bhakti tradition influence

## Literary Significance
- Bridge between Eastern and Western literary traditions
- Influenced modern poetry globally
- Established Tagore's international reputation
