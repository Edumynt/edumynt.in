# Rabindranath Tagore (1861-1941)

Rabindranath Tagore was a Bengali polymath who became the first non-European to win the Nobel Prize in Literature in 1913.

## Early Life
- Born in Calcutta to the prominent Tagore family
- Educated at home by private tutors
- Early exposure to literature, music, and art

## Literary Achievements
- **Gitanjali** (1910) - Poetry collection that won him the Nobel Prize
- Over 2000 songs composed
- Founder of Visva-Bharati University

## Philosophy
- Advocated for universal humanism
- Believed in synthesis of Eastern and Western cultures
- Promoted education through creative expression
