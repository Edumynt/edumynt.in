# Indian Writers in English Literature

This course explores the rich tradition of Indian writers who have contributed significantly to English literature, from the colonial period to contemporary times.

## Course Scope

- **Colonial Era**: Early pioneers who wrote in English
- **Post-Independence**: The flourishing of Indian English literature
- **Contemporary**: Modern voices and global recognition

## Major Themes

- Identity and belonging
- Cultural conflict and synthesis
- Social reform and critique
- Postcolonial consciousness
