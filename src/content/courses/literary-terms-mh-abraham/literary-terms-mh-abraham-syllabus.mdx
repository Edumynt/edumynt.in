# Literary Terms By MH Abraham

A comprehensive study of literary terminology, figures of speech, and critical concepts based on M.H. Abraham's acclaimed work on literary terms and criticism.

## Course Modules:

### 1. Figures of Speech
- Comprehensive study of rhetorical devices
- Poetic and prose techniques
- Examples and applications

### 2. Literary Terms
- Essential terminology for literary analysis
- Critical concepts and definitions
- Usage in literary criticism

### 3. Important Glossary
- Key terms and definitions
- Reference material for literature students
- Quick lookup guide

### 4. Literary Periods
- Historical overview of literary movements
- Period characteristics and features
- Timeline and context
