# Putting It All Together — Pattern Detective

## Opening Hook

You've learned all 7 patterns. Now it's time to prove you can use them.

In real life, sentences don't come labeled. You need to figure out the pattern yourself. You're a **Pattern Detective** now.

---

## The 7 Patterns at a Glance

| # | Pattern | Structure | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | SV | Subject + Verb | "Birds fly." |
| 2 | SVO | Subject + Verb + Object | "She reads books." |
| 3 | SVOO | S + V + Indirect O + Direct O | "She gave me a gift." |
| 4 | SVC | S + Linking V + Complement | "She is a teacher." |
| 5 | SVOC | S + V + O + Object Complement | "They elected him president." |
| 6 | SVA | S + V + Required Adverbial | "He lives in Mumbai." |
| 7 | SVOA | S + V + O + Required Adverbial | "She put the book on the shelf." |

---

## The Detective's Method

1. **Find the Subject** — Who/what is this about?
2. **Find the Verb** — What does the subject do?
3. **What follows the verb?** → Determines the pattern
4. **Verify** — Use the linking verb test, completeness test, etc.

---

## Detective Cases

Identify the pattern:

1. "The sun rises in the east." → **SVA** (rises where? required)
2. "I consider this a mistake." → **SVOC** (this = mistake)
3. "She told me the truth." → **SVOO** (me = receiver, truth = thing)
4. "The flowers are beautiful." → **SVC** (flowers = beautiful)
5. "He kicked the ball into the goal." → **SVOA** (kicked it where? required)
6. "Time flies." → **SV** (simplest pattern)
7. "My mother cooked dinner." → **SVO** (cooked what?)

---

## Error Correction

Fix the structural errors:

1. "She book reads." → "She reads a book." (SVO — fix word order)
2. "He gave to me a gift." → "He gave me a gift." (SVOO — don't mix forms)
3. "The food tastes deliciously." → "The food tastes delicious." (SVC — adjective, not adverb)
4. "They elected president." → "They elected him president." (SVOC — add object)
5. "She put the keys." → "She put the keys on the table." (SVOA — add adverbial)
6. "I happy am." → "I am happy." (SVC — English word order)
