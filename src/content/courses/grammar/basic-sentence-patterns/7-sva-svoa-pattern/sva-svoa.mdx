# Adding Where/When/How — Adverbial Patterns (SVA & SVOA)

## Opening Hook

Read these:
- "He lives." — Feels incomplete! WHERE?
- "She put the book." — Incomplete! WHERE?

Now:
- "He lives in Mumbai." — Complete!
- "She put the book on the shelf." — Complete!

Some verbs NEED a place, time, or manner to make sense. These required additions are called **adverbials**.

---

## What Are Adverbials?

Adverbials tell us:
- **Where?** — "in Mumbai," "on the table"
- **When?** — "every morning," "at 5 PM"
- **How?** — "quickly," "with care"
- **Why?** — "because of the rain"

**Optional vs Required:**

> **Think about it:** Remove the word in bold from each sentence. Does it still feel complete?
> 1. "She sings **beautifully**." → "She sings." ✅ Complete → **optional**
> 2. "He lives **in Mumbai**." → "He lives." ❌ Incomplete → **required**
> 3. "She put the book **on the shelf**." → "She put the book." ❌ Incomplete → **required**

---

## SVA Pattern (Subject + Verb + Required Adverbial)

| Subject | Verb | Required Adverbial |
|---|---|---|
| He | lives | in Mumbai |
| The meeting | is | at 3 PM |
| She | went | to the market |

```
🔵 Subject + 🔴 Verb + 🟣 Required Adverbial
```

**Verbs that usually need adverbials:** live, go, stay, be (location), arrive.

---

## SVOA Pattern (Subject + Verb + Object + Required Adverbial)

| Subject | Verb | Object | Required Adverbial |
|---|---|---|---|
| She | put | the book | on the shelf |
| He | placed | the letter | in the envelope |
| I | threw | the ball | to him |

```
🔵 Subject + 🔴 Verb + 🟢 Object + 🟣 Required Adverbial
```

**Verbs that need object + adverbial:** put, place, keep, throw, drive, send.

> **Think about it:** "She put the book" — something's missing! WHERE did she put it? "Put" ALWAYS needs a place.

---

## Special Case: "There is/are" (Existential Sentences)

English uses "there is/are" to show that something exists:
- "There is a book on the table." = "मेज़ पर एक किताब है"
- "There are many students in the class."

Hindi doesn't have this structure. Hindi puts the thing first: "मेज़ पर एक किताब है" (On table a book is).

---

## Hindi Connection
Hindi puts adverbials BEFORE the verb. English puts them AFTER.
- Hindi: "वह मुंबई में रहता है" (Subject + Adverbial + Verb)
- English: "He lives in Mumbai." (Subject + Verb + Adverbial)

## Practice

**Required or optional?**
1. "She sings beautifully." → **Optional**
2. "He lives in London." → **Required**
3. "She put the keys in the drawer." → **Required**

**Identify the pattern:**
1. "The children played in the garden." → **SVA**
2. "She put the umbrella by the door." → **SVOA**
3. "He is a teacher." → **SVC**
4. "They elected her captain." → **SVOC**

## Key Takeaways
1. Adverbials tell where, when, how, why.
2. SVA: Some verbs need an adverbial (live, go, stay).
3. SVOA: Some verbs need object + adverbial (put, place, throw).
4. Use the completeness test to identify required adverbials.
5. "There is/are" = existential pattern (unique to English).
