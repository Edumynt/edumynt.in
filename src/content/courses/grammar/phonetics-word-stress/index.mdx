# Phonetics & Word Stress

Master English pronunciation — from individual sounds to word stress to sentence rhythm.

This chapter covers the fundamentals of English phonetics and word stress. You'll learn how speech sounds are produced, how to read IPA symbols, how to apply word stress rules, and how to fix the most common pronunciation errors Hindi speakers make.

## What You'll Learn

| Lesson | Topic | What You'll Master |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | The Sound System | Phonetics basics, organs of speech, IPA |
| 2 | Consonant Sounds | Place/manner of articulation, problem sounds |
| 3 | Vowel Sounds | English vowel system, schwa, diphthongs |
| 4 | Word Stress (Basics) | Stress rules for 2-syllable words |
| 5 | Word Stress (Advanced) | Suffix-based rules, stress shifts |
| 6 | Sentence Stress & Rhythm | English rhythm, connected speech |
| 7 | Problem Sounds | Fixing common Hindi speaker errors |
| 8 | Pronunciation Detective | Analyzing any word independently |

## Why This Matters

English pronunciation is notoriously difficult for Hindi speakers because:
- English spelling is **not phonetic** (unlike Hindi)
- English has **more vowel sounds** than Hindi
- English **word stress** changes meaning
- English **rhythm** is completely different from Hindi

This chapter gives you the tools to overcome all of these challenges.
