# Grammar

Grammar becomes easier when you stop memorizing isolated rules and start noticing structure.

This course begins with sentence patterns and clause analysis because those two chapters help you answer the questions that matter most while writing:

- What kind of structure does this verb need?
- What is missing from this sentence?
- Which part is the subject, object, complement, or adverbial?
- Where does Hindi structure pull me away from natural English?

## What You'll Learn

| Chapter | What You'll Learn to Do |
|---|---|
| Basic Sentence Patterns | Build and recognize the core English sentence patterns used in everyday writing |
| Phonetics & Word Stress | Hear and produce clearer English speech, especially where Hindi sound habits interfere |
| Clause Analysis in Terms of SPOCA | Break clauses into Subject, Predicator, Object, Complement, and Adverbial with confidence |

## Who This Course Is For

- Hindi-speaking learners who understand some English but struggle to build accurate sentences
- Learners preparing for exams who need grammar as a usable system, not a list of definitions
- Writers and speakers who want to correct their own errors more independently

## How the Lessons Work

- **Example first** — you start with real sentences before formal labels
- **Hindi-aware explanation** — lessons point out where Hindi and English organize meaning differently
- **Practice inside the lesson** — you keep testing your judgment instead of only reading explanations
- **Depth over slogans** — the goal is not to sound clever about grammar, but to use it well
