# Grammar

Master English grammar from the ground up.

This course covers the foundational structures of English, starting with **basic sentence patterns** — the 7 templates that every English sentence follows. From there, you'll build up to more advanced grammar concepts.

## What You'll Learn

| Chapter | What You'll Master |
|---|---|
| Basic Sentence Patterns | The 7 fundamental sentence structures of English |
| Clause Analysis in Terms of SPOCA | Subject, Predicator, Object, Complement, and Adverbial analysis |

## Who Is This For?

- Hindi-speaking learners who want to build correct English sentences
- Anyone who knows basic English but struggles with grammar
- Learners preparing for competitive exams (UGC NET, UPSC, etc.)

## How It Works

- **Intuition first** — Discover patterns through examples, not rules
- **Hindi comparisons** — Every lesson shows how Hindi and English differ
- **Active learning** — "Think about it" moments keep you engaged
- **Practice-heavy** — For every 10 minutes of explanation, 10 minutes of practice
